Paths, outlines, range rings, trails and gizmos are line segments. The lines feature draws them
1px or at any pixel width, in a colour or a pulse through up to four, solid or dashed. A line is
built once or refilled every frame without allocating, and it lives on its own or with an entity.
The engine's debug drawing (ray helpers, collider wireframes, the LOD and spatial overlays) is
drawn with it.
