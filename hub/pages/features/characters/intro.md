A character is a physics body that walks: a dynamic, upright rigid body moved by its velocity,
with gravity and contacts left to the physics engine. It walks, runs, crouches and jumps, slides
down slopes too steep to stand on, rides moving platforms, and tumbles and gets up again. A
brain drives it (a player's keys or gamepad, an AI), or your code does, through the same intent.
