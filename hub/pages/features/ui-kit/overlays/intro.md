The pieces that open over the HUD: [windows](#windows) the player moves, resizes and closes,
modal [dialogs](#dialogs), [toasts](#toasts), and the [tooltips](#tooltips),
[popovers](#popovers) and [menus](#menus-and-split-buttons) anchored to a control. They share one
set of rules for [Escape and the focus](#escape-focus-and-stacking): Escape closes the topmost
overlay only, and a closed overlay gives the focus back to what had it. Each section has a live
demo: its buttons open the piece over this page, as it opens over your game's canvas.
