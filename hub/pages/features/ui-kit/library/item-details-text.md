## Item details

`createItemDetails({ item, compareTo })` is an item's name, rarity, description and stats, each
compared with another item's (the one in hand) by an arrow, a sign and the difference: better in
the success role, worse in the danger role, and said in words ("5 less, worse"). The content for a
slot's tooltip, or a panel beside an inventory that follows the focused slot (a gamepad hovers
nothing).

- **States:** in the page: alone, compared, empty, and in a slot's tooltip.
