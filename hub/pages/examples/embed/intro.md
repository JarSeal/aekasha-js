::: embed banner
The banner, mounted into this page. It's the smallest build an embed can be: one scene made in
code, no engine feature, {{sbp:banner.initial}} of gzip before its first frame.
:::

An embed puts the engine into an element of a page it doesn't own: a site's hero, a product
view, a background. The page keeps its own styles, layout and input. This example walks through
the banner from its scene to the page that shows it.
