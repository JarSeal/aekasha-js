## Progress bar

[What it is and its states](hub:features/ui-kit/library#progress-bar), on the public page.

The indeterminate bar's screenshot shows the sliding bar mid-track: the box's checks pause its
slide at a fifth of its time (`pauseAt: 0.2`, p804 D13). The slide eases out fast, so at the
default 40 % the bar is past the right edge, and at its start it's off the left one.
