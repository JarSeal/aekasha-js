## Patterns

::: note To decide
- The menus: pause, settings, load and save, and how the player moves through them (mouse,
  keyboard, gamepad).
- Confirmations: what the game asks before (quitting, overwriting a save, spending something
  rare), and what it lets the player undo instead.
- Loading: the loading screen and what it shows (progress, tips), and what happens on a slow
  load.
- Errors the player can meet (a failed save, a lost connection) and what each one says and offers.
:::

Start from: [Ækasha's patterns](hub:project/aekasha/design#patterns).
