The app's plans in `docsApp/plans/` and its issues in `docsApp/issues/`, read when the Hub
builds, in the same shape as [Ækasha's roadmap](hub:project/aekasha/roadmap). An app plan
follows Ækasha's plan rules with a number space of its own, and it can wait on an Ækasha plan,
named by its repo path, while an Ækasha plan never names an app plan:
[the app's plans and issues](repo:docs/plans/CLAUDE.md#the-apps-plans-and-issues) has the
differences.
