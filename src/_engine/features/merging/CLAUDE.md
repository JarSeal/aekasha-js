## Mesh merging

The `merging()` feature (`MergingFeature.ts`, p372, docs/plans/p372_mesh-merge-groups.md; the epic is p370): mesh entities that share a material drawn as one merged mesh (a merge group), each member staying an entity with its own transform, physics body, data and asset refs. It's built on p371's `mergeGeometryParts` (`kernel/assets/GeometryMerge.ts`, imported by relative path, loaded on the first build). The template lists it last of the twelve, after `streaming()` (after `physics()` matters: see The scene step).

- **Files.** `MergeGroups.ts` (the API, the component hooks, the builds, the scene step, the install), `MergeState.ts` (the per-world maps: groups, pending builds, the queue, the load's holds; `createGroup`, `markPending`, `syncMemberPose`, `releaseMember`), `MergeInPlace.ts` (a member's changes in place: `rewriteMember`, `dropMember`, `collapseDisabledMember`, `restoreEnabledMember`) on `MergeRanges.ts` (the buffer writes), `MergeAuto.ts` (the `merge` queue and the `AUTO` buckets), `MergeSceneSettings.ts` (a scene's `mergeGroups`, the defaults, the streaming warnings), `MergeEligibility.ts` (the refusals), `MergingSchema.ts` (the schema part), `MergeTypes.ts`, `debug/_dbg__MergeGroups.ts` (the moved-member check). `MergeGroups.ts` is near the 800 lines: new state goes to `MergeState.ts`.
- **Components.** `MERGE_GROUP` (on the group entity: `id`, `members`, `settings`, `partIndex`, `wasted`, `rebuilds`), `MERGE_MEMBER` (`groupEntityId`, `groupId`, `partIndex`, -1 until built) and `TAG_MERGED` are kernel component types (`kernel/ecs/ECSCoreComponents.ts`, the data types `import type` from `MergeTypes.ts`); `TAG_MERGED` is the sixth reason in `reconcileObject3DVisibility` (after `TAG_DRAW_HELD`), its hooks `merging()`'s. A member gets `MERGE_MEMBER` when it joins, so `getMergeGroupOf` answers before the build; `TAG_MERGED` and leaving the DEFAULT spatial domain (its `SPATIAL_INDEXED` removed, given back when it leaves) at the build that includes it.
- **The group entity** is managed (`MANAGED_BY { manager: 'MERGE', ownerId: <scene id>, role: 'group' }`, appId `mergeGroup:<id>`) and goes with the scene it was created in, unless `persistent` (`PERSISTENT`; only a persistent group takes persistent members). It has no mesh until its first build, which adds `OBJECT3D` and `TAG_IS_MESH` (so `disposeMesh` releases its refs on delete), the material's ref (the first member's, `MergeGroupData._material`) and `SPATIAL_INDEXED`. The mesh sits at the centre of the members' world bounds with the members baked relative to it: the spatial index measures a mesh's radius from its origin. Each build re-joins the spatial index, so the radius is measured again. The geometry is registered as `mergeGroup:<id>` (`#<rebuilds>` appended only while something still holds the previous one), never persistent; a rebuild releases the previous one.
- **API** (`aekasha/merging`; each throws without the feature, naming it): `createMergeGroup({ id, castShadow?, receiveShadow?, persistent? }, world?)` (the shadow flags default to the first member's; a taken id, `'AUTO'` and an `auto:` id throw), `addToMergeGroup(groupId, entityIds, world?)` (makes the group on first use, with its scene entry's settings; `'AUTO'` queues them as the `merge` prop does), `removeFromMergeGroup`, `updateMergeMember`, `rebuildMergeGroup`, `dissolveMergeGroup` (deletes the group entity, whose `onDeleteEntity` lets the members go) and `getMergeGroupOf`. `buildPendingMergeGroupsAsync(world?)` (`@internal`) builds what's pending now, worker builds awaited.
- **Builds.** `mergeGroupBuildSystem` (`APP_RENDER_SYNC`, `APP_RENDER_SYNC_ORDER.MERGE_BUILD` 0.5, before the pose producers) first joins the queued `merge`s, then builds the pending groups once a frame. Its first run with a pending group only starts the merge code's `import()`; the next run builds. Up to `WORKER_BUILD_VERTICES` (65 536, `@internal`) the merge runs on the main thread, above it in the assets worker (`mergeGeometryPartsAsync`), the new members drawn on their own until it's in. A worker result is dropped when a newer build started (`_buildToken`), the group was deleted or a member left (then the group is marked again). A build that finds no member left deletes an `AUTO` group and takes an explicit one's mesh out of the scene. The builds read a member's pose from its `TRANSFORM` (`syncMemberPose`), both storage modes: `object3DSyncSystem` copies it only in `MAIN`.
- **In place** (`MergeInPlace.ts`): `updateMergeMember` rewrites the member's range from its `TRANSFORM` (positions; normals by the normal matrix; tangents by the linear part, a merge-computed tangent turned by the change since `MERGE_MEMBER._bakedMatrix`; `w` and the winding flipped when the mirroring changed), one `addUpdateRange` per attribute written, no rebuild, no allocation. The bounds only grow (box and sphere) until the next build; the group re-joins the spatial index only when they grew. A running worker build is dropped and started again; a member whose geometry's vertex count changed warns and makes its group build again. `removeFromMergeGroup` and a deleted member collapse the member's index range to its first vertex and count its vertices in `wasted`; past `COMPACT_WASTE_SHARE` (25 % of the merged vertices) the group is marked for a compacting build. Collapsed ranges are still submitted (degenerate triangles) until then. `DISABLED` collapses a built member's range without counting it as waste (`_isCollapsed`), enabling writes it back from the member's geometry.
- **Remerge is explicit**, never driven by `TRANSFORM` dirty flags: members are static by contract, and a dirty flag is set by writes that aren't moves (a fixed body's one-off sync, a tier-frozen body's settling sync). In the debug env, `mergeMoveCheckSystem` (`LATE_MAIN`, every `MOVE_CHECK_FRAMES` 60 frames; loaded through `ctx.onDebug`) compares each built member's world matrix with `_bakedMatrix` (1e-5 per element) and warns once per member, naming `updateMergeMember`.
- **The `merge` prop** (`MeshProps.merge?: MeshMergeDef`, `kernel/mesh/Mesh.ts`, the type `import type`): `'AUTO'`, a group id, or `{ group?, cellSize?, maxVertices? }` (`group` defaulting to `'AUTO'`, the sizes only for `AUTO`). `createMeshEntity` hands it to the handler the install sets (`setMeshMergeHandler`, `@internal`, as `setAutoLodResolverLoader`); without `merging()` it warns once and the mesh is drawn on its own. The handler queues it (`queueMerge`: an empty id, an `auto:` id or a size that isn't a finite number over 0 warns and is dropped; a later `merge` for the entity replaces it), and the mesh joins at the next build (`collectQueuedJoins`, in creation order), so the refusals and `AUTO`'s cell see the final entity (an import's bodies, a move right after creation). A mesh whose physics creation is pending (`TAG_IS_PHYSICS_OBJECT` without `COLLIDER`: `createPhysicsEntity` adds the `BODY_*` after an `await`) stays queued, so a dynamic body is refused rather than drawn frozen. A queued entity deleted meanwhile is dropped; `addToMergeGroup` with another id wins over a queued one, `removeFromMergeGroup` drops one silently.
- **`AUTO`** (`pickAutoGroup`): the key is the material's registered id (else its uuid), the attributes' names and item sizes, both shadow flags, the layers' mask, `PERSISTENT` (its group is then persistent) and the cell size; the cell is x/z only (`worldToCell`, `utils/CellKey.ts`, of the world position at the resolution). The id is `auto:<FNV-1a of the key, base 36>:<cx>,<cz>:<n>`. A bucket counts the vertices that joined its open group (a removed member isn't taken off: the next group opens early, never late), and a group's first member goes in whatever its size. When its open group is gone, the bucket goes back to the first id after the full groups still alive, so a scene's next load gets the same ids. A refused mesh warns (`merge 'AUTO': …`) before a bucket is picked. Defaults: `DEFAULT_AUTO_CELL_SIZE` 32 m, `DEFAULT_AUTO_MAX_VERTICES` 262 144.
- **Schema and scene settings.** `mergingSchemaPart` (`MergingSchema.ts`, on `aekasha/schemas`, last in the gatherer's `ENGINE_SCHEMA_PARTS`): a mesh's `merge` and a scene's `mergeGroups` (an `{ id: 'AUTO', cellSize?, maxVertices? }` entry, the scene's `AUTO` defaults, or a group's `{ id, castShadow?, receiveShadow? }`, strict; an id listed twice fails; no `persistent`), so the feature manifest names `merging`. `MergeSceneSettings.ts` keeps each scene's entries (`sceneRegistered` hook) and its JSON `streaming.cellSize`; the scene read is the loading one, else the current one. A group made on first use takes its entry's settings; `createMergeGroup`'s options don't read the scene.
- **Streaming** (warnings, once per scene): an `AUTO` cell size that doesn't divide the scene's streaming cell size, and a build whose members are in two streaming cells. Only the JSON's `streaming` is seen (merging imports nothing of streaming's). A group doesn't load, unload, prime or fade with a cell: a streamed member that goes collapses in place, a cell loaded during play draws its members on their own (primed and held by streaming) until the next frame's build, and the group mesh isn't primed (its first draw may compile).
- **Imported assets** (no import extension: `spawnImportedAsset` creates its pieces with `createMeshEntity`): the `merge` custom prop (`kernel/assets/import/CustomProps.ts`, a trimmed string on `ParsedCustomProps.merge`; `customPropTemplates.blend` has no `merge` prop yet) and `SpawnImportedParams.merge`. A piece's `merge` is its `meshProps` entry's, else its node's prop, else the spawn's, which leaves out every node of a physics group whose rigid node isn't `FIXED` (a node's own prop there is kept, and refused when it joins).
- **The scene step** (`installMergeGroups`' scene hooks): `loadStart` marks a load running (`setMergeSceneLoading`), and the build system does nothing meanwhile; `entered` is `buildSceneMergeGroupsAsync` (p372 §6): it joins the queue and builds every pending group, worker builds awaited, up to three rounds, then ends the load's state and lets go of the holds; `loadSettled` does the last two for a load that failed before `entered`. A failed build is logged (the load goes on) and the build system takes it. A mesh that joins during a load gets `TAG_DRAW_HELD` (`holdForSceneLoad`) unless something else holds it, and the step lets go of only its own holds: a scene's members are never drawn on their own, so their own geometry is never uploaded. Listed after `physics()`, its `entered` runs after physics settled the scene's bodies; listed before, a member whose body isn't created yet stays queued past the step and the build system takes it.
- **Refusals** (`getMergeRefusal`, a warning naming the entity and the reason; the entity is drawn on its own): not a plain mesh (skinned, instanced or batched, a pool's instance, morph targets, no 3-component position), a material array (p374), a transparent material, another material than the group's, a dynamic or kinematic body or a `PHYSICS_SIM_TIER` (a tier-frozen dynamic body), `MANAGED_BY`, `LOD` (until p376), a group already, a persistent entity in a group that isn't, an attribute whose item size differs from the group's. The checks read three's type flags (`isSkinnedMesh`, `isBatchedMesh`, …) with three as a type import: an `instanceof THREE.BatchedMesh` pulled `BatchedMesh` (+4.6 KiB gzip) into the app's first download.
- **Integration.** The group mesh is culled whole by three (its bounding sphere) and is the spatial index's entry; a merged member's own culling flags (`ecsFrustumCullingEnabled`) and its LOD do nothing. Bodies stay per member (colliders are never merged). Raycasts hit the group mesh (mapping a face to its member is p373's `getMergeMemberAtFace`).
- **Tests.** `MergeGroups.test.ts` (builds, placement, refs, in-place updates against a fresh merge, collapses and compaction, disabling, the scene exits, the refusals, worker builds held and dropped with the async merge mocked, the system's lazy load and order), `MergeAuto.test.ts` (the queue, `AUTO`'s key, cells, caps and ids, the scene settings and the streaming warnings, pending bodies, the scene step and its holds through the kernel's scene hooks), `MergingSchema.test.ts`, `debug/_dbg__MergeGroups.test.ts`; the kernel's `Mesh.test.ts`, `CustomProps.test.ts` and `SpawnImported.test.ts` for the seams. In a scene: `testDebugScene` (`p372_grid`: 200 boxes in one explicit group; the `AUTO` lattice: 500 boxes, 3 materials, 4 cells, 12 groups), `largeWorld` (409 members in 28 `AUTO` groups: its props and ruins) and `exampleMerging`.
- **SBP.** `_engine/features/merging` is budgeted at 6 KiB gzip in the app's first download (`sbp.config.ts`; 6.0 kB after p372 Phase 3, with `utils/CellKey.ts`); `GeometryMerge.js` stays a lazy chunk. An app without `merging()` carries only the kernel's part (the three component keys, the reconcile's check, `MERGE_BUILD`, the `merge` prop's handler seam).
