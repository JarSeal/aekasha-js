## Mesh merging

The `merging()` feature (`MergingFeature.ts`, p372, `docs/plans/_DONE_p372_mesh-merge-groups.md`; the epic is p370): mesh entities that share a material drawn as one merged mesh (a merge group), each member staying an entity with its own transform, physics body, data and asset refs. It's built on p371's `mergeGeometryParts` (`kernel/assets/GeometryMerge.ts`, imported by relative path, loaded on the first build). The template lists it last of the twelve, after `streaming()` (after `physics()` matters: see The scene step).

- **Files.** `MergeGroups.ts` (the API, the component hooks, the build system, the scene step, the install), `MergeBuild.ts` (the builds: the pending groups dispatched by mode to their code, loaded on its first use; the `BAKED` build), `MergeBatch.ts` (the `BATCHED` build, p375: a lazy chunk with `BatchedMesh`) and `MergeBatchFront.ts` (its first-download part: the loader, a leaving member, the visibility hooks), `MergeState.ts` (the per-world maps: groups, pending builds, the queue, the load's holds; `createGroup`, `markPending`, `syncMemberPose`, `releaseMember`), `MergeInPlace.ts` (a member's changes in place: `rewriteMember`, `dropMember`, `collapseDisabledMember`, `restoreEnabledMember`) on `MergeRanges.ts` (the buffer writes), `MergeAuto.ts` (the `merge` queue and the `AUTO` buckets), `MergeSceneSettings.ts` (a scene's `mergeGroups`, the defaults, the streaming warnings), `MergeEligibility.ts` (the refusals), `MergePalettes.ts` (palette groups' look-ups, p374), `MergingSchema.ts` (the schema part), `MergeTypes.ts`, `MergeMemberEdit.ts` (edit sessions and picking, p373 D4), `debug/_dbg__MergeGroups.ts` (the moved-member check); the debugger's modules are Merge debugging's. p375 S2 moved the builds out of `MergeGroups.ts` (796 lines then, 550 after): new state goes to `MergeState.ts`, new logic to the module it's about.
- **Components.** `MERGE_GROUP` (on the group entity: `id`, `members`, `settings`, `partIndex`, `wasted`, `rebuilds`), `MERGE_MEMBER` (`groupEntityId`, `groupId`, `partIndex`, -1 until built; `_editPose` while an edit session is open) and `TAG_MERGED` are kernel component types (`kernel/ecs/ECSCoreComponents.ts`, the data types `import type` from `MergeTypes.ts`); `TAG_MERGED` is the sixth reason in `reconcileObject3DVisibility` (after `TAG_DRAW_HELD`), its hooks `merging()`'s. A member gets `MERGE_MEMBER` when it joins, so `getMergeGroupOf` answers before the build; `TAG_MERGED` and leaving the DEFAULT spatial domain (its `SPATIAL_INDEXED` removed, given back when it leaves) at the build that includes it.
- **The group entity** is managed (`MANAGED_BY { manager: 'MERGE', ownerId: <scene id>, role: 'group' }`, appId `mergeGroup:<id>`) and goes with the scene it was created in, unless `persistent` (`PERSISTENT`; only a persistent group takes persistent members). It has no mesh until its first build, which adds `OBJECT3D` and `TAG_IS_MESH` (so `disposeMesh` releases its refs on delete), the material's ref (the first member's, `MergeGroupData._material`; a palette group's merge material, set when it's made: see Merge materials) and `SPATIAL_INDEXED`. The mesh sits at the centre of the members' world bounds with the members baked relative to it: the spatial index measures a mesh's radius from its origin. Each build re-joins the spatial index, so the radius is measured again. The geometry is registered as `mergeGroup:<id>` (`#<rebuilds>` appended only while something still holds the previous one), never persistent; a rebuild releases the previous one.
- **API** (`aekasha/merging`; each throws without the feature, naming it): `createMergeGroup({ id, castShadow?, receiveShadow?, persistent?, palette? }, world?)` (the shadow flags default to the first member's; a taken id, `'AUTO'`, an `auto:` id, and a `palette` that isn't registered or with `persistent` throw), `addToMergeGroup(groupId, entityIds, world?)` (makes the group on first use, with its scene entry's settings; `'AUTO'` queues them as the `merge` prop does), `removeFromMergeGroup`, `updateMergeMember`, `rebuildMergeGroup`, `dissolveMergeGroup` (deletes the group entity, whose `onDeleteEntity` lets the members go) and `getMergeGroupOf`; the edit sessions' `beginMergeMemberEdit`, `endMergeMemberEdit`, `cancelMergeMemberEdit` (these three throw without the feature; the getters don't), `isMergeMemberInEdit` and `getMergeMemberAtFace` (see Edit sessions). `buildPendingMergeGroupsAsync(world?)` (`@internal`) builds what's pending now, worker builds awaited.
- **Builds.** `mergeGroupBuildSystem` (`APP_RENDER_SYNC`, `APP_RENDER_SYNC_ORDER.MERGE_BUILD` 0.5, before the pose producers) first joins the queued `merge`s, then builds the pending groups once a frame. Its first run with a pending group only starts the merge code's `import()`; the next run builds. Up to `WORKER_BUILD_VERTICES` (65 536, `@internal`) the merge runs on the main thread, above it in the assets worker (`mergeGeometryPartsAsync`), the new members drawn on their own until it's in. A worker result is dropped when a newer build started (`_buildToken`), the group was deleted or a member left (then the group is marked again). A build that finds no member left deletes an `AUTO` group and takes an explicit one's mesh out of the scene. The builds read a member's pose from its `TRANSFORM` (`syncMemberPose`), both storage modes: `object3DSyncSystem` copies it only in `MAIN`.
- **In place** (`MergeInPlace.ts`): `updateMergeMember` rewrites the member's range from its `TRANSFORM` (positions; normals by the normal matrix; tangents by the linear part, a merge-computed tangent turned by the change since `MERGE_MEMBER._bakedMatrix`; `w` and the winding flipped when the mirroring changed), one `addUpdateRange` per attribute written, no rebuild, no allocation. The bounds only grow (box and sphere) until the next build; the group re-joins the spatial index only when they grew. A running worker build is dropped and started again; a member whose geometry's vertex count changed warns and makes its group build again. `removeFromMergeGroup` and a deleted member collapse the member's index range to its first vertex and count its vertices in `wasted`; past `COMPACT_WASTE_SHARE` (25 % of the merged vertices) the group is marked for a compacting build. Collapsed ranges are still submitted (degenerate triangles) until then. `DISABLED` collapses a built member's range without counting it as waste (`_isCollapsed`), enabling writes it back from the member's geometry.
- **Edit sessions** (`MergeMemberEdit.ts`, p373 D4): moving a member without a write per frame. `beginMergeMemberEdit` records the `TRANSFORM`'s pose in `MERGE_MEMBER._editPose` (through `syncMemberPose`, either storage mode; a second begin keeps the first pose; a non-member warns), collapses a built member's range (`collapseDisabledMember`'s write, `_isCollapsed`, no waste) and takes its `TAG_MERGED` away, so its own mesh draws it (`object3DSyncSystem` keeps that at the `TRANSFORM`); a member waiting for its build only records the pose. The caller moves it as it likes (`setTransform`); nothing runs per frame. `endMergeMemberEdit` applies the `TRANSFORM`'s position and rotation with `world.setTransform` (a fixed body doesn't follow its mesh), writes the range (`updateMergeMember`'s write, a running worker build dropped), restores the index with the new pose's winding unless disabled (`restoreEnabledMember`) and gives `TAG_MERGED` back, even when disabled (an enable later restores the index only) and not while its group is shown unmerged; without a session it does nothing. `cancelMergeMemberEdit` writes the begin pose (`commitTransform`) and ends. The session is the component's field, so a removed or deleted member's session goes with it. The guards that ship (the only part in the first download: `MergeMemberEdit.ts` is tree-shaken unless an app calls it): `applyBuild` leaves a member in a session without `TAG_MERGED` and collapses it after the build (in its member loop: a build during a session bakes the mid-move pose, End writes the final one), and `restoreEnabledMember` skips it (an enable mid-session would draw it twice); a disable mid-session hides its own mesh and leaves the range collapsed. A scene exit cancels every open session in every world (`registerOnAllSceneExits`, registered on the first begin: no module side effect), so a persistent member gets its pose back.
- **Picking** (`getMergeMemberAtFace(groupEntityId, faceIndex, world?)`): a raycast's `object.userData.entityId` is the group entity; a binary search of the group geometry's `mergeParts` by `indexStart` for `faceIndex × 3`, the part's `key` (the member's entity id) checked against the member's `partIndex`, so a removed member's range answers undefined, as does a group not built. No allocation.
- **Remerge is explicit**, never driven by `TRANSFORM` dirty flags: members are static by contract, and a dirty flag is set by writes that aren't moves (a fixed body's one-off sync, a tier-frozen body's settling sync). In the debug env, `mergeMoveCheckSystem` (`LATE_MAIN`, every `MOVE_CHECK_FRAMES` 60 frames; loaded through `ctx.onDebug`) compares each built member's world matrix with `_bakedMatrix` (1e-5 per element) and warns once per member, naming `updateMergeMember`; a member in an edit session is skipped.
- **The `merge` prop** (`MeshProps.merge?: MeshMergeDef`, `kernel/mesh/Mesh.ts`, the type `import type`): `'AUTO'`, a group id, or `{ group?, cellSize?, maxVertices?, palettes? }` (`group` defaulting to `'AUTO'`, the rest only for `AUTO`). `createMeshEntity` hands it to the handler the install sets (`setMeshMergeHandler`, `@internal`, as `setAutoLodResolverLoader`); without `merging()` it warns once and the mesh is drawn on its own. The handler queues it (`queueMerge`: an empty id, an `auto:` id, a size that isn't a finite number over 0 or a `palettes` that isn't a list of ids warns and is dropped; a later `merge` for the entity replaces it), and the mesh joins at the next build (`collectQueuedJoins`, in creation order), so the refusals and `AUTO`'s cell see the final entity (an import's bodies, a move right after creation). A mesh whose physics creation is pending (`TAG_IS_PHYSICS_OBJECT` without `COLLIDER`: `createPhysicsEntity` adds the `BODY_*` after an `await`) stays queued, so a dynamic body is refused rather than drawn frozen. A queued entity deleted meanwhile is dropped; `addToMergeGroup` with another id wins over a queued one, `removeFromMergeGroup` drops one silently.
- **`AUTO`** (`pickAutoGroup`): the key is the material's registered id (else its uuid; a palette member's `palette:<id>`, see Merge materials), the attributes' names and item sizes, both shadow flags, the layers' mask, `PERSISTENT` (its group is then persistent) and the cell size; the cell is x/z only (`worldToCell`, `utils/CellKey.ts`, of the world position at the resolution). The id is `auto:<FNV-1a of the key, base 36>:<cx>,<cz>:<n>`. A bucket counts the vertices that joined its open group (a removed member isn't taken off: the next group opens early, never late), and a group's first member goes in whatever its size. When its open group is gone, the bucket goes back to the first id after the full groups still alive, so a scene's next load gets the same ids. A refused mesh warns (`merge 'AUTO': …`) before a bucket is picked. Defaults: `DEFAULT_AUTO_CELL_SIZE` 32 m, `DEFAULT_AUTO_MAX_VERTICES` 262 144.
- **Schema and scene settings.** `mergingSchemaPart` (`MergingSchema.ts`, on `aekasha/schemas`, last in the gatherer's `ENGINE_SCHEMA_PARTS`): a mesh's `merge` and a scene's `mergeGroups` (an `{ id: 'AUTO', cellSize?, maxVertices?, palettes? }` entry, the scene's `AUTO` defaults, or a group's `{ id, castShadow?, receiveShadow?, palette? }`, strict; an id listed twice fails; no `persistent`), so the feature manifest names `merging`. `MergeSceneSettings.ts` keeps each scene's entries (`sceneRegistered` hook, `AUTO`'s `palettes` as `autoPalettes`) and its JSON `streaming.cellSize`; the scene read is the loading one, else the current one. A group made on first use takes its entry's settings; `createMergeGroup`'s options don't read the scene.
- **Streaming** (warnings, once per scene): an `AUTO` cell size that doesn't divide the scene's streaming cell size, and a build whose members are in two streaming cells. Only the JSON's `streaming` is seen (merging imports nothing of streaming's). A group doesn't load, unload, prime or fade with a cell: a streamed member that goes collapses in place, a cell loaded during play draws its members on their own (primed and held by streaming) until the next frame's build, and the group mesh isn't primed (its first draw may compile).
- **Imported assets** (no import extension: `spawnImportedAsset` creates its pieces with `createMeshEntity`): the `merge` custom prop (`kernel/assets/import/CustomProps.ts`, a trimmed string on `ParsedCustomProps.merge`; `customPropTemplates.blend` has no `merge` prop yet) and `SpawnImportedParams.merge`. A piece's `merge` is its `meshProps` entry's, else its node's prop, else the spawn's, which leaves out every node of a physics group whose rigid node isn't `FIXED` (a node's own prop there is kept, and refused when it joins).
- **The scene step** (`installMergeGroups`' scene hooks): `loadStart` marks a load running (`setMergeSceneLoading`), and the build system does nothing meanwhile; `entered` is `buildSceneMergeGroupsAsync` (p372 §6): it joins the queue and builds every pending group, worker builds awaited, up to three rounds, then ends the load's state and lets go of the holds; `loadSettled` does the last two for a load that failed before `entered`. A failed build is logged (the load goes on) and the build system takes it. A mesh that joins during a load gets `TAG_DRAW_HELD` (`holdForSceneLoad`) unless something else holds it, and the step lets go of only its own holds: a scene's members are never drawn on their own, so their own geometry is never uploaded. Listed after `physics()`, its `entered` runs after physics settled the scene's bodies; listed before, a member whose body isn't created yet stays queued past the step and the build system takes it.
- **Refusals** (`getMergeRefusal`, a warning naming the entity and the reason; the entity is drawn on its own): not a plain mesh (skinned, instanced or batched, a pool's instance, morph targets, no 3-component position), a material array (one draw per material: split it into a mesh per material, which a palette can merge; p374 open question 4), a transparent material, another material than the group's (a palette group's: one its palette doesn't have), a dynamic or kinematic body or a `PHYSICS_SIM_TIER` (a tier-frozen dynamic body), `MANAGED_BY`, `LOD` (until p376), a group already, a persistent entity in a group that isn't, an attribute whose item size differs from the group's. The checks read three's type flags (`isSkinnedMesh`, `isBatchedMesh`, …) with three as a type import: an `instanceof THREE.BatchedMesh` pulled `BatchedMesh` (+4.6 KiB gzip) into the app's first download.
- **The mode** (p375, `docs/plans/p375_batched-mesh-batches.md`, Phase 1 in progress): `MergeGroupSettings.mode` (`'BAKED'`, the default, all of the above; `'BATCHED'`: the members as instances of one `BatchedMesh`) and `perMemberCulling` (`BATCHED` only, default true), on `createMergeGroup`, a scene's group entry (the schema refuses `perMemberCulling` without `BATCHED`, and `palette` with it) and `MeshMergeOptions.mode`. A group made on first use takes its scene entry's mode, else the first joining `merge`'s that names one (`collectQueuedJoins` returns them as `modes`; `joinMergeGroup` is `addToMergeGroup` with them); a `merge` naming the other mode is refused. `createMergeGroup` throws for an unknown mode and a `BATCHED` palette group. `BATCHED`'s refusals (`getMergeRefusal`'s `requestedMode` for `AUTO` before a bucket): bodies and tiers allowed; a mesh not directly under the root scene refused (its matrix is its `TRANSFORM`'s); a geometry's index presence, attribute names, item sizes and normalization must be the group's exactly (`BatchedMesh` throws on the first ones and drops an extra attribute). `AUTO`: `BATCHED` is in the key (a `BAKED` key unchanged, so the pre-p375 ids stay; `MergeBatch.test.ts` pins one), palettes ignored, and the cap counts each geometry once per open group (`AutoBucket.geometries`). **The batch build** (`MergeBatch.ts`, loaded behind `import()` by the build system's first run with a pending batched group, `startPendingBuildCodeLoads`; the scene step awaits it, `loadPendingBuildCodeAsync`; `buildPendingGroups` leaves a group whose mode's code isn't in pending): the first build, and one `rebuildMergeGroup` asks for (`requestBatchRebuild`), makes a `BatchedMesh` from every member, its geometries in first-seen order and each geometry's members a block of instance ids, `sortObjects` off (D1), `perObjectFrustumCulled` from `perMemberCulling`, sized with 25 % room (`BATCH_RESERVE`, plus 16 instances), replacing a previous batch in the group's `OBJECT3D` (disposed), its visibility reconciled (a disabled or held group's new batch stays hidden). A later build adds only the members without an instance, growing the buffers when full (`setGeometrySize`, `setInstanceCount`). The batch sits at its members' bounds' centre from when it was made (the group's `TRANSFORM` too); an instance's matrix is its member's world matrix less that centre (`writeBatchMatrix`). Its internal geometry gets the batch's bounding sphere and box after each build (`updateBatchBounds`): a `BatchedMesh` is a mesh to the spatial index (`TAG_IS_MESH` from its `isMesh`), which measures it by its geometry's sphere; the group re-joins the index when the radius grew. Members get `MERGE_MEMBER.instanceId` (`partIndex` stays -1, so every baked in-place path and the moved-member check skip them), `TAG_MERGED` (not while the Merging tab shows the group unmerged, debug env only, as `applyBuild`), and leave the spatial index as baked ones do. Leaving (`dropBatchMember`) deletes the instance; the freed id is reused lowest first by the next join (S4's grouping). The group entity's deletion disposes the batch through `disposeMesh`. Visibility (`installBatchMemberVisibility`): `DISABLED`, `TAG_FRUSTUM_CULLED` and `TAG_DRAW_HELD` hooks set the instance's `setVisibleAt` (`isBatchMemberShown`, with the hook's override), a scene load's held members shown when the step lets go. `updateMergeMember` does nothing for a batched member, and `beginMergeMemberEdit` warns. **The sync** (`mergeBatchSyncSystem`, `APP_RENDER_SYNC` at the kernel's `APP_RENDER_SYNC_ORDER.MERGE_BATCH_SYNC` -0.25: after the pose producers, before the consumers; added to a world with its first batch, `addSystem` ignoring it after): per world's batches (`batchesByWorld`, a deleted or replaced batch dropped at its next run), it writes an instance from its member's own mesh (position, quaternion, scale: a member is directly under the root scene), so the batch draws a member where its mesh would be drawn on its own, interpolated bodies included and a `TRANSFORM` written after `MAIN` a frame later, as an own mesh. It writes when `OBJECT3D._lastVersion` changed since the last write (`MERGE_MEMBER._batchVersion`): the transform's version in `MAP` storage, and in `TYPED_ARRAY` a count `object3DSyncSystem` bumps with each copy (kernel, p375 S3: without it, a write the copy's cleared dirty flag hid wasn't seen); a `BODY_DYNAMIC_VISUAL` member every frame (interpolation moves its mesh without a version). Storages read once, no allocation. Each write grows the batch's sphere to hold the member (`growBatchSphere`: grow-only, its geometry's copy too; a full build measures it again), and a batch that grew re-joins the spatial index once that frame. **Grouping and compaction** (D1, S4): r187 merges adjacent draws of one geometry in instance id order, a freed or hidden id between them skipped. A batch keeps its own geometry id per instance id (`BatchState.geometryAt`, -1 freed; three's is private), marked dirty by every add and delete; the sync then counts the runs and the geometries in use (`countRuns`) and, past `COMPACT_RUNS_FACTOR` (2) times the geometries, compacts that frame (`compactBatch`): the live ids, in order, each given the next member by geometry id then old id, through `setGeometryIdAt`, its matrix (its own mesh's) and visibility written again. No texture or buffer is reallocated (deleting and re-adding would be: `addInstance` sorts the freed ids each call, and `setInstanceCount` remakes the textures); the frame allocates its lists. A leave alone never splits a run; a join into a freed id can. Both backends compact (WebGL2's multi-draw doesn't need it, and it costs little). `getBatchState(group)` has `runs`, `geometriesInUse` and `compactions`. The tests check the count against three's own (`_updateDrawParameters(…, 'merged')`, `_mergedDrawCount`: 5 draws to 2). The debugger: Merge debugging's "Batched groups". `MergeBatch.test.ts` is at about 800 lines: new tests go to a file of their own. Tests: `MergeBatch.test.ts` (its first test needs the batch code unloaded: keep it first), `MergingSchema.test.ts`.
- **Integration.** The group mesh is culled whole by three (its bounding sphere) and is the spatial index's entry; a merged member's own culling flags (`ecsFrustumCullingEnabled`) and its LOD do nothing. Bodies stay per member (colliders are never merged). Raycasts hit the group mesh; `getMergeMemberAtFace` maps the hit's face to its member (see Picking).
- **Tests.** `MergeGroups.test.ts` (builds, placement, refs, in-place updates against a fresh merge, collapses and compaction, disabling, the scene exits, the refusals, worker builds held and dropped with the async merge mocked, the system's lazy load and order), `MergeAuto.test.ts` (the queue, `AUTO`'s key, cells, caps and ids, the scene settings and the streaming warnings, pending bodies, the scene step and its holds through the kernel's scene hooks), `MergeMemberEdit.test.ts` (sessions across a build, a worker build, a disable, a remove, a delete and a scene exit; picking by raycast), `MergingSchema.test.ts`, `debug/_dbg__MergeGroups.test.ts` and the debugger's (see Merge debugging); the kernel's `Mesh.test.ts`, `CustomProps.test.ts` and `SpawnImported.test.ts` for the seams. In a scene: `testDebugScene` (`p372_grid`: 200 boxes in one explicit group; the `AUTO` lattice: 500 boxes, 3 materials, 4 cells, 12 groups), `largeWorld` (409 members in 28 `AUTO` groups: its props and ruins) and `exampleMerging`.
- **SBP.** `_engine/features/merging` is budgeted at 8 KiB gzip in the app's first download (`sbp.config.ts`, 7 KiB until p373 Phase 3): 6.0 kB after p372 Phase 3, 6.9 kB once an app calls `updateMergeMember` or `removeFromMergeGroup` (`exampleMerging` does; until then the in-place writes were tree-shaken), 7.2 kB with p373's debug figures' calls and A/B check (both folded) and the edit sessions' two guards, plus `utils/CellKey.ts`. `MergeMemberEdit.ts` isn't in it until an app calls a session function (the debugger's import is in its lazy chunk). `GeometryMerge.js` stays a lazy chunk. An app without `merging()` carries only the kernel's part (the three component keys, the reconcile's check, `MERGE_BUILD`, the `merge` prop's handler seam).

## Merge debugging

p373 (`docs/plans/_DONE_p373_merge-debug-and-member-editing.md`): the Merging drawer tab, the group and member windows, the overlay, the A/B view and the member window's pose fields on edit sessions. All of it is in `debug/` (`_dbg__` modules, the default world), loaded by `registerMergeDebug` (`merging()`'s install: `if (IS_DEBUG_ENV) ctx.onDebug(registerMergeDebug)`, with the moved-member check), so a production build carries none of it (`yarn verify:baselines`' debug code check).

- **The figures the merging code keeps for it** (`MergeState.ts`, `@internal`, each call behind `if (IS_DEBUG_ENV)`, so a build folds them): `getMergeDebugStats(world)` → `{ builds, inPlaceUpdates, lastBuild, refusals }` from `noteMergeBuild` (`buildGroup` times a build from its start to applied; a worker build's time includes the wait), `noteMergeInPlaceUpdate` (the four in-place writes) and `noteMergeRefusal` (`addToMergeGroup`'s, `AUTO`'s and an invalid `merge`'s; a later refusal of the same entity replaces its record; the newest `MAX_MERGE_REFUSALS`, 200), and the A/B view's set (`getMergeUnmergedViews`, `isMergeGroupShownUnmerged`).
- **The tab** (`_dbg__Merging.ts`, `mergingControls`, last in `DEFAULT_DEBUG_DRAWER_TAB_ORDER`; the built-in `merge` icon, `svg/merge-group.svg`, not on the `aekasha/ui` entry; `refreshIntervalMs` 500, `keepOnSceneChange`: everything is read through getters). A summary pane of monitors: groups (drawn, pending, shown unmerged), members (merged, waiting, queued), merged and wasted vertices, draws per pass, main and shadow, "N now · M unmerged" (before culling: what the pass submits with everything in view; a light's own passes, cascades and cube faces, aren't counted), builds and in-place updates in the last second with the totals (`createMergeRateMeter`), the last build, the refused count, then "Highlight all groups" and the cap's note. The group list (`mergeGroups`, the tab's first list: the id, `[entityId]`, an `AUTO` and a `BATCHED` badge, the material and cell, members, vertices (a batched group's: members, visible members, runs, compactions); a row opens the group window, its toggles highlight it and show it unmerged, the second hidden on a group not built). A closed pane "Refused entities" (`mergeRefusals`: newest first, those alive and in no group, with the reason). Its reads are `_dbg__MergingInfo.ts` (`getMergeSummary`, `getStandingRefusals`, the names: an entity's fixed app id, else its debug name, else "Entity N"), which the windows share.
- **The windows** (`_dbg__MergingWindows.ts`, kinds `mergeGroup` and `mergeMember`, registered at the module's load, `removeOnSceneChange`: entity ids don't outlive a scene; `createDebugWindowGrid`, refreshed every 250 ms). The group's: its state, kind, material, both shadow flags, persistent, members, vertices, wasted vertices (a rebuild compacts: every build merges the live members only, so Rebuild is the compact button too), builds, the highlight and "Show unmerged" checkboxes, Rebuild and Dissolve (reasoned buttons), the members as a kit list (at most 200 rows, a row's suffix its state unless merged; a row opens the member window), the entity's info rows. The member's: "Merged into" with a button opening the group's window, its state (merged, waiting for its group's build, being moved, disabled, drawn on its own while shown unmerged), geometry, material, vertices, its part, the pose fields, "Remove from group" (reasoned: not while a session is open), the entity's info rows. `_registerMergingWindowLinks()` (called by the tab's creation, the boot's debug step: `registerEntityWindowOpener` is a no-op until the environment says the profiler loads) registers `_registerManagerDebugInfo('MERGE', { label: 'Merge group', icon: 'merge', tabId: 'mergingControls' })` and the profiler's openers (`mergeGroup` at the default priority, `mergeMember` at -1, after physics' and the character's).
- **The pose fields** (`_dbg__MergingMemberPose.ts`, `getMergeMemberPoseItems` → `{ items, onRemove }`): Position, Rotation (degrees; the shown Euler kept while the quaternion is the one it made, within 1e-6) and Scale, each `{ get, set }` on the `TRANSFORM`. A `set` opens an edit session if none is open and writes (`setTransform`; the scale with `commitTransform`); `onChange` with `last: true` ends it, records one `merge.memberTransform` (nothing when the pose didn't change) and refreshes the tab; the grid's `onRemove` ends a session a gesture left open. Scale is read-only with a body (colliders don't scale). The kit's defaults: no step or precision (the arrows step 1 m).
- **Undo** (`_dbg__MergingUndo.ts`, handlers registered at the module's load, `'perScene'`): `merge.dissolve` (undo makes the group again with its id, kind and settings, an `AUTO` one through `createGroup` as its bucket made it, without touching `MergeAuto.ts`' buckets, and adds back the members still alive), `merge.removeMember` (undo adds the member back, making its group again when it went) and `merge.memberTransform` (the pose before and after; undo and redo apply it, then `updateMergeMember` when it's still a member and not in a session). **Entries carry a scene visit token** (new on every page load and scene exit): members are named by entity id, which is another entity after a reload or on another visit, so an entry from another visit warns and does nothing; a deleted entity warns too.
- **The overlay** (`_dbg__MergingOverlay.ts`, as LOD's): `MERGE_OVERLAY_COLORS`, 8 colours, one 1 px `THIN` line each (`MERGE_DEBUG_OVERLAY_<n>`, `persistent`, `FIXED`, made on first use, `markDebugHelper`); a group's colour is `hashStringFNV1a(group.id)` modulo 8, the same on every visit and after a rebuild. A member's box is its geometry's local box by the matrix it's drawn with (`_bakedMatrix` once built; its own world matrix while it waits or is in a session), refilled every frame in `APP_RENDER_SYNC` (at `LOD_SELECTION`'s order: after the camera's final pose, before the render) while anything is highlighted, and once at a toggle (a paused app loop doesn't run the system). Only in view (the active camera's frustum, the debug camera's too): a built group outside is skipped, then each member box outside; a disabled group or member isn't outlined. `MAX_BOXES_PER_COLOR` 1024: past it a box is counted, not written, and the summary says how many weren't drawn. Highlighted by "all" or by group id (a rebuild or an `AUTO` group made again keeps it; turning one off under "all" turns "all" off and keeps the others); the per-group ids clear on scene exit, "all" stays. Without `lines()`: one log line (`isLinesInstalledFor`) and the checkbox reads off again.
- **Show unmerged** (`_dbg__MergingUnmerged.ts`, the A/B): only a built group (`OBJECT3D`'s add hook doesn't run the visibility reconcile, so a hold made before the first build wouldn't hide the mesh it adds). On: the group entity gets `TAG_DRAW_HELD` (only when nothing held it already, and only that hold let go) and its built members lose `TAG_MERGED` (drawn on their own, their own culling back); `applyBuild` keeps a shown group's members so (behind `IS_DEBUG_ENV`) and its first-merge guard reads `partIndex === -1` from before the build, so a rebuild during the view keeps `_wasSpatialIndexed`. Off: `TAG_MERGED` back on the built members not in a session, the hold let go. Views are by group entity and end at scene exit (a persistent group's too); a dissolve, removal or deletion during one needs nothing (`releaseMember`). No undo (a view, not an edit). In the browser (p373 Phase 2): the image unchanged but where members' coplanar faces touch, the draws up by the members per group and pass (less their own culling).
- **Batched groups** (p375 S5; the mode is Mesh merging's "The mode"). The figures read a batch through the loader (`getMergeBatchModule()?.getBatchState`, `getGroupBatchState` and `getMergeBatchFigures` in `_dbg__MergingInfo.ts`), so the debugger's chunk doesn't pull `BatchedMesh` in. "Built" is `isMergeMemberBuilt` (a part or an instance). The summary counts a batched group's members' vertices (what its instances draw, `getGroupVertexCount`; its batch holds each geometry once) and its geometry runs as its draws (a draw each on WebGPU; WebGL2 draws it in one; one before the sync's first count). The tab's row: a `BATCHED` badge (`AUTO · BATCHED` for an `AUTO` one; a `BAKED` group has none) and "N members (V visible), R runs, C compactions", visible being `getVisibleAt` (disabled, held or culled by the engine; three's own per-member culling is per pass, not counted). The group window: a Mode row (both modes), and for a batched group Batch (geometries, used of reserved vertices, instances of room), Draw runs (with the geometries in use and the compactions) and Per-member culling in place of Palette and Wasted vertices. The member window: its state by its instance (merged, shown unmerged, disabled, held or frustum-culled: its instance hidden), its Part as "instance #i, geometry #g", and the pose fields without an edit session (the sync draws its moves; one undo entry per gesture, from the first write's pose; Scale read-only with a dynamic body too). The overlay outlines it at its own mesh's pose composed now (its matrixWorld is the last render's; the batch sync runs before the overlay), and culls a batched group whole only when every member has an instance (`instanceCount`). Show unmerged holds the batch and takes `TAG_MERGED` off its members as for a baked group; the batch build keeps a shown group's members so, and a full build's new batch is hidden by the hold. The moved-member check skips a batched member (`partIndex` -1).

## Merge materials

p374 (`docs/plans/_DONE_p374_multi-material-merging.md`, implemented): a palette (`*.mergeMaterial.json`) of `STANDARD` materials drawn by one merge material, so meshes of different materials can share one draw: a merge group on a palette (explicit, or `AUTO`'s by palette).

- **The asset and the gather.** `MergeMaterialSchema.ts` (`MergeMaterialAssetSchema`, on `aekasha/schemas`): 2-256 member material ids and the slots `map`, `normalMap`, `orm` and `emissiveMap`, each with its `size` and `missing` (`NEUTRAL` or `SKIP`). The class check (`devTools/assetPipeline/mergeMaterialClass.ts`: one shading model and state, the maps the slots take) and the resolver (`devTools/assetPipeline/mergeMaterials.ts`) build one array per slot a member uses (the texture `<paletteId>.<slot>`, layer `i` member `i`'s map or a neutral layer) and bake the table's entries (`__entries`) and the members' agreed state (`__state`). A scene lists its palettes (`mergeMaterials`, `mergingSchemaPart`): the gather inlines each as a `SceneMergeMaterial` and adds its slot arrays to the scene's `textures`, so production carries a palette only for a scene that lists it.
- **The load.** `merging()`'s `assetsLoaded` scene hook (`createSceneMergeMaterialsAsync`, `MergeMaterialLoad.ts`, the only part in the first download): on the first scene that lists a palette it loads `MergeMaterialRegister.ts` (one lazy chunk with `MergeMaterial.ts`), then registers each palette's merge material under the palette's id (the materials' id space) and its table as the texture `<paletteId>.table`, both owned by the scene, before the scene code runs: the scene code and a JSON mesh's `mat` find it. The next scene listing the palette shares it (re-tagged) while it samples the same loaded arrays; arrays loaded again make a new one. A slot array that didn't load warns (every member samples its neutral value there) or fails the load with `throwOnError`; another material under the palette's id fails the load.
- **The material** (`createMergeMaterial`): a `MeshStandardNodeMaterial`. The vertex's palette index is `aekMatIndex` (`mergeGeometryParts` writes it from each part's `paletteIndex`: `Uint8`, `Uint16` past 255, `gpuType = IntType`), read through a flat varying. The table is an RGBA32F `DataTexture`, one row per entry of `MERGE_TABLE_TEXELS` (5) texels read with `textureLoad` (colour and opacity; emissive and intensity; roughness, metalness, normal scale; the UV rows with the AO intensity and the `SKIP` flags), one path on both backends. Each slot's array is sampled at the entry's UV transform with `sampleArrayLayer`, and the sockets read them as a `STANDARD` material reads its maps (ORM: AO r, roughness g, metalness b). A slot with no array gives every member its neutral value. A `SKIP` slot samples in an `If` on the entry's flag, with `.grad()` from the UV's derivatives taken outside it: those explicit gradients pick slightly other mip levels than an implicit sample (the verification scene's figures, below). The table and arrays are in `userData.uniforms` (where the scene's asset release, streaming's priming and the GPU memory tab look), its `MergeMaterialInfo` in `userData.mergeMaterial` (`getMergeMaterialInfo`).
- **Palette groups** (`MergePalettes.ts`, in the first download; p374 Phase 3, D4). A group's `palette` (`createMergeGroup`, a scene's `mergeGroups` group entry) names a palette the scene lists: the gather fails on one it doesn't (`checkMergeGroupPalettes`, `devTools/gather/mergeMaterials.ts`), `createMergeGroup` throws when no merge material is registered under the id (`findPaletteMaterial`: the registered material whose `MergeMaterialInfo` names that palette) and for `palette` with `persistent` (the merge material and its arrays are the scene's). The group's `_material` is the merge material from its creation (`createGroup`). It takes a mesh whose material is the registered instance of one of the palette's ids (`getPaletteIndex`: a variant, `<id>#<hash>`, or a clone carrying the id is another material, refused naming the palette); the index is kept on `MERGE_MEMBER._paletteIndex` at the join, and every build passes it as the part's `paletteIndex`, so the merge writes `aekMatIndex` (a group of one material passes none: no attribute). A group made on first use from a scene entry whose palette isn't registered is logged by the queue's joins (`resolveQueuedMerges`), its meshes drawn on their own. A member's parameter overrides from a scene's save entry aren't in the table.
- **`AUTO` by palette.** A scene's `AUTO` entry's `palettes` (default none) and a mesh's `merge.palettes` (replacing the scene's list; `[]` none): `pickAutoGroup` keys a mesh whose material is in one of them (the first in list order that has it, `findMeshPalette`) by `palette:<id>`, so a cell's materials of the palette share its group, made with the palette. A persistent mesh ignores them. A palette with no merge material registered warns once per scene and palette (`warnPaletteNotRegistered`) and is skipped; the gather fails on an `AUTO` palette the scene doesn't list and warns when two share a material.
- **What it costs** (p374 D5, measured on the verification scene's field, Apple M2, Chrome; the plan has the table): a palette trades pixels for draws. By palette 10 draws against 37 by material, but the merge material costs 0.6-0.8 ms more GPU time a frame with the field from above and 1.8-1.9 ms with it filling the view, on both backends, while the 27 draws saved about 0.03 ms of CPU (a draw's JavaScript is about 1 µs). Not bisected: the table's five texel reads, every slot sampled and normal-mapped for every member, the array sampling. Memory: the merged geometry gains `aekMatIndex` (4 bytes a vertex on WebGPU, where three widens the `Uint8` to a `Uint32Array` on upload; 1 on WebGL2), and a palette group, its materials' pieces together, passes 65 535 vertices sooner, where `mergeGeometryParts`' index goes from 16 to 32 bits (`maxVertices: 65535` keeps it at 16, no GPU time lost); at one texture size the arrays cost what the members' own textures do. The Hub's Mesh merging page ("When a palette pays") and `docs/techniques/terrain-modular-kits.md` (Variations) give the "when not" list.
- **API.** `palette` on `createMergeGroup` (`MergeGroupSettings`) and `palettes` on `MeshMergeOptions`; `SceneMergeMaterial`, `MergeMaterialEntry` and `MergeMaterialSlotInfo` are types on `aekasha/merging`. `createMergeMaterialAsync(palette, tableId)` (public since engine 6.7.0; `@internal` until then, which the Hub warned about) makes one from palette data, unregistered, for a variant of a listed palette without a second palette JSON: the verification scene's `SKIP` variant, a copy of its palette's data. A variant registered under another id draws only the meshes given it: groups find a palette's merge material by the palette's id. The Merging tab's group window shows a group's palette, the member window its palette index.
- **Tests.** `MergeMaterial.test.ts` (the slots, the packing, the sockets, the state), `MergeMaterialLoad.test.ts` (registration, owners, sharing, reloads, missing arrays, a taken id, `createMergeMaterialAsync`), `MergePalettes.test.ts` (the look-ups, palette groups and their refusals and throws, a scene entry's palette, `AUTO`'s palettes: 36 groups to 9), `MergeMaterialSchema.test.ts`; the gather's in `devTools/assetPipeline/` and `devTools/gather/gather.test.ts`. In a scene: `mergeMaterials` (a debug scene, `src/app/scenes/mergeMaterials/`, an orthographic camera): `ruinsPalette` on eight meshes drawn as `REFERENCE` (the members' own materials), `BAKED` (one geometry with a group per member, the four materials as an array: the control), `MERGED` (the palette's merge material) and `SKIP` (`normalMap` and `orm` as `SKIP`); `window.__mergeMaterials.show(variant)` shows one alone at the origin, so a screenshot of `MERGED` diffs against `BAKED`'s, and `results.draws` counts each one's draws. Its field (`mergeMaterialsField.ts`, D5's test scene): 1 600 meshes, 400 per member, on 3 × 3 `AUTO` cells of 8 m (the scene's `AUTO` entry, `palettes: ["ruinsPalette"]`), seen by a camera of its own: `field('PALETTE' | 'PALETTE_16' | 'MATERIAL' | 'UNMERGED' | 'NONE')` shows one (`PALETTE_16`: `merge: { maxVertices: 65535 }`, two groups a cell with 16-bit indices; `MATERIAL`: `merge: { palettes: [] }`) and resolves with its meshes, groups and draws, `results.field` has each mode's (in prod test 9 groups and 10 draws by palette, 18 and 19 capped, 36 and 37 by material, 1 601 draws unmerged, on both backends; the debug env's helpers add 2 from above), and `fieldView('OVERVIEW' | 'CLOSE')` switches its camera (from above, or low over its edge where the meshes fill the frame: D5's per-pixel figures).
