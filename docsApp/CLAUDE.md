The app's own plans (`docsApp/plans/`) and issues (`docsApp/issues/`), kept apart from Ækasha's in `docs/` so an app made from the template keeps its own without mixing them with the engine's. They use `docs/`' formats: a plan follows `docs/plans/CLAUDE.md` (its "The app's plans and issues" says what differs: the numbers, the references between the two folders), an issue `hub/CLAUDE.md`'s issue-file convention. A change to `src/app/` or the app-level `src/*.ts` files is the app's; one to the engine or the toolkit is Ækasha's, even when an app scene shows it. The `.gitkeep` files keep the empty folders in the template.
